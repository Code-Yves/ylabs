Axis Modular SIP Series · A-401 — Interior Partition

Rev. 02 · 2026-04-19 · ISSUED FOR CONSTRUCTION
A-401: Interior partition 38×89 studs @ 406 o.c. · 12.7 mm Type X gyp both sides · STC ≥ 45 · Expand for full spec
  • Wood studs 38×89 mm SPF @ 406 mm o.c. (16″), balloon-framed floor-to-deck.
  • Bottom plate: single 38×89 on closed-cell sill gasket; shot to subfloor / blocking.
  • Top plate: double 38×89 lap-jointed at partition intersections.
  • Cavity insulation: 89 mm mineral-fibre batt (friction-fit, no gaps/compression).
  • Facing: 12.7 mm Type X gypsum both sides (moisture-resistant at bath wet-face).
  • Fasteners: 32 mm drywall screws, 200 mm o.c. perimeter, 300 mm o.c. field.
  • Acoustic sealant: continuous bead at perimeter, penetrations, and back-to-back boxes.
  • Target rating: STC ≥ 45 (tested); add resilient channel one side to reach STC 50 at bath wall.
  • Fire: partition is not a required fire separation in 1-dwelling-unit module.

Technical Note — Code & Reference

  • OBC 9.10.9 — Fire separations (reference only; no separation required within a single dwelling unit).
  • OBC 9.11 — Sound control between dwelling units (not applicable within unit; STC 45 is an Axis internal spec).
  • OBC 9.23 — Wood-frame construction, stud spacing and plate requirements.
  • OBC 9.29 — Gypsum board installation, fastener schedules, and joint treatment.

Axis Modular SIP Series · A-102 — Roof Plan (TPO Membrane + Parapet)

Rev. 02 · 2026-04-19 · ISSUED FOR CONSTRUCTION
A-102: Roof plan — 42'-0" × 12'-0" low-slope TPO over parapet · ¼ in/ft drainage to two scuppers · Expand for full spec
  • Roof assembly: 10.25" (260 mm) roof SIP panel, R-40 effective; continuous 305 mm wood-framed parapet rim on all four sides; see A-305 for parapet section, A-304 for field section.
  • Membrane: 1.5 mm fully-adhered TPO, white reflective surface (min. solar reflectance R-3 equivalent); heat-welded seams; bonding adhesive per manufacturer's written instructions; galvanized coping cap over parapet.
  • Drainage: central ridge high-point at module C.L.; ¼ in/ft (2%) min slope both directions per OBC 9.26.14; two primary scuppers (100 × 75 mm) on long walls plus two secondary overflow scuppers 50 mm above primary invert.
  • Drainage capacity: ~24 m² roof area; sized for 15-minute rainfall intensity per OBC Div. B Appendix C data (Ottawa/GTA range); confirm local rainfall intensity per site municipality.
  • Skylight: 610 × 1220 mm (2'×4') curb-mounted unit on 150 mm site-built insulated curb with self-adhered air barrier counterflashing; glazing per CSA A440.2; centred over living/kitchen on the drainage ridge.
  • Vents: 75 mm ABS plumbing vent stack above bath core; 150 mm HRV exhaust and 150 mm HRV intake separated ≥3000 mm horizontally per OBC 9.32.3.9; 150 mm range-hood exhaust over kitchen. All penetrations pre-manufactured TPO boots, heat-welded, with self-adhered air barrier flashing at the deck transition.
  • Mechanical: mini-split condenser pad 900 × 400 mm on 50 mm EPDM isolation layer, NE corner; line-set penetration through parapet with pitch pan; see M-601.
  • Lightning protection: Class I air terminals at each of the four parapet corners (optional per owner's election); bonded to building ground per CSA B72 where provided.

Technical Note — Code & Reference

  • OBC 9.26 — Roofing materials and low-slope assemblies (general requirements).
  • OBC 9.26.14 — Roof drainage; ¼ in/ft (2%) minimum slope on low-slope roofs; primary and overflow drainage required.
  • OBC 9.26.4 — Low-slope roofing membrane systems (continuous, sealed, mechanically or fully adhered).
  • OBC 9.26.6 — Flashing at parapet, penetrations, scuppers, and terminations.
  • OBC 9.25 — Heat transfer, air leakage, condensation control; self-adhered air barrier continuity at roof/wall transition.
  • OBC 9.32.3.9 — HRV intake and exhaust separation (≥3000 mm horizontal; intake located to avoid contaminant sources).
  • OBC 9.10.19 — Smoke/CO alarms n/a on roof plane; noted for attic/ceiling assemblies per A-201.
  • CSA A440.2 — Fenestration energy performance (applies to skylight unit).
  • All penetration boots, scupper liners, and coping details to use continuous self-adhered air barrier + compatible TPO flashing; no brand-specified products listed — substitutions permitted per spec.

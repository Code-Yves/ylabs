Rev. 02 · 2026-04-19 · ISSUED FOR CONSTRUCTION
S-102: Pier layout — 8 helical piers in 4 × 2 grid; 3200 o.c. long-axis, 1830 o.c. short-axis · Expand for full spec
  • Pier count: 8 total, arranged in 4 columns (A–D) × 2 rows (1–2) beneath the 12800 × 3658 mm modular footprint.
  • Spacing: 3200 mm o.c. long-axis (3 interior bays at 3200 + 400 mm end overhang west / 2800 mm overhang east to module ends); 1830 mm o.c. short-axis with 914 mm symmetric edge offset.
  • Helical pier: 168 mm OD shaft, single helix plate per CCMC 13193-R evaluation; allowable axial capacity ≥ 30 kN each (pier-level design to be verified by engineer of record).
  • Embedment: 2400 mm minimum to frost line (OBC 9.12.2.2); Ontario frost depth 1200–1500 mm typical — site-confirm per OBC 9.12.2.2 Table.
  • Chassis: HSS 150 × 100 × 6.4 long-axis beams along both pier rows (see S-301); HSS 100 × 100 × 6.4 cross beams at each bay division (1/4-points and ends).
  • Installation: torque-tested to minimum 2 500 ft-lb at final seat; Kt (torque-to-capacity) factor per CCMC 13193-R listed pile evaluation — confirm active listing and site geotechnical report.
  • Service penetration zone: centered between bays B and C (row midline) — accommodates HDPE water supply, ABS waste/vent stack, and electrical conduit rise.
  • Steel grade: CSA G40.21 350W; hot-dip galvanized per CSA G164; welding qualifications to CSA W47.1 / CSA W59.

Technical Note — Code & Reference

  • OBC 9.12.2.2 — Foundation/pier embedment below frost line; site frost depth per Table 9.12.2.2 (verify per local climate-zone data); minimum 2400 mm to underside of frost-susceptible strata.
  • OBC 9.15.1.3 — Alternative/engineered foundations; helical piles on engineered design by P.Eng.
  • OBC 9.23.6 — Anchorage of the building to foundation (pier bracket to HSS chassis).
  • CCMC 13193-R — Helical pier evaluation listing (verify active status before specifying).
  • CSA G40.21 350W — Structural steel grade for HSS chassis and pier brackets.
  • CSA G164 — Hot-dip galvanizing of irregularly shaped articles (piers, brackets, HSS).
  • CSA W47.1 / CSA W59 — Fabrication, welding qualification and weld procedures.

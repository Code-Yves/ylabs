M-602: HRV airflow · supply 30 L/s · exhaust 30 L/s · balanced ±10% · intake/exhaust ≥1 m apart · Expand for full spec
  • Single-module HRV in mech closet; cross-flow core; MERV 8 filter both streams.
  • Supply air to bedroom (15 L/s) and living/kitchen (15 L/s) = 30 L/s total.
  • Exhaust air from bathroom (15 L/s) and kitchen (15 L/s) = 30 L/s total.
  • Balance supply / exhaust within ± 10%, verified at commissioning per CSA F326.
  • Rigid galvanized sheet-metal ducts, sealed (UL 181) and insulated in unconditioned spaces.
  • Flex duct limited to ≤ 1.5 m at equipment connection only.
  • Exterior intake and exhaust hoods separated ≥ 1000 mm, screened, min 450 mm AFG.
  • Defrost: recirculation-type, activates ≤ −5 °C outdoor air temperature.
  • Balance dampers at each branch for commissioning.

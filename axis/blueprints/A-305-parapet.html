Axis Modular SIP Series · A-305 — Parapet + Coping

Rev. 02 · 2026-04-19 · ISSUED FOR CONSTRUCTION
A-305: Parapet with continuous membrane turn-up & HDG metal coping · Expand for full spec
  • Wall SIP extends up past roof line to form parapet; min. 600 mm above finished roof membrane.
  • Inside-corner cant strip (45° PT wood) eliminates 90° bend of membrane at roof-to-parapet junction.
  • Roofing membrane continuous: horizontal run → cant → up parapet inside face → over top → down exterior behind metal coping.
  • Parapet top blocking: 2-ply PT softwood, sloped 2% toward interior to shed water onto roof.
  • Metal coping: HDG steel, hemmed drip on both edges, spliced joints with backup splice plates and butyl tape.
  • Concealed fasteners only: continuous HDG cleat on exterior face; screw-and-neoprene washers on interior face at 600 mm o.c. max.
  • Backer rod + low-modulus PU sealant at coping/cladding interface.
  • Min. 200 mm vertical leg of membrane above finished roof per industry practice; termination bar optional.

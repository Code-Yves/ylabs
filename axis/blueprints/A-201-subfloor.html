Axis Modular SIP Series · A-201 — Subfloor Assembly

Rev. 02 · 2026-04-19 · ISSUED FOR CONSTRUCTION
A-201: Subfloor assembly — 360 mm total depth, R-24 cavity, U ≈ 0.237 W/m²·K · Expand for full spec
  • Finish: 5 mm click-lock luxury vinyl plank on 1 mm IXPE foam underlay; Class I abrasion; floating installation.
  • Subfloor: 18 mm OSB T&G panel, glued & screwed to joist top flange; fastener schedule 150 mm at edges / 300 mm in field per CSA O437.
  • Vapour barrier: 6-mil polyethylene, continuous, sealed at all penetrations on the warm-in-winter side per OBC 9.25.4.
  • Structure: 302 mm engineered wood I-joist @ 406 mm o.c.; mineral-fibre batt R-24 friction-fit in cavity.
  • Thermal: R_SI = R-24 × 0.1761 ≈ 4.23 m²·K/W; U ≈ 0.237 W/m²·K (meets OBC 9.36.2.6 Climate Zone 6 min. for floors over unheated spaces).
  • Soffit: 16 mm gypsum board on under-joist sheathing; taped and sealed at perimeter to form continuous air barrier per OBC 9.25.3.
  • Assembly depth total: 360 mm from F.F. to soffit underside.

Technical Note — Code & Reference

  • OBC 9.25.3 — Air barrier systems (continuity at floor/wall junctions).
  • OBC 9.25.4 — Vapour barriers on warm-in-winter side.
  • OBC 9.25.5 — Thermal insulation continuity.
  • OBC 9.36.2.6 — Effective thermal resistance of floor assemblies, Climate Zone 6.
  • CSA O437 — OSB sheathing grade for subfloor application.
  • U-value derivation: U [W/m²·K] = 1 / (R_imp × 0.1761).

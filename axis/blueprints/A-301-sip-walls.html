Axis Modular SIP Series · A-301 — SIP Wall Panel

Rev. 02 · 2026-04-19 · ISSUED FOR CONSTRUCTION
A-301: 165 mm SIP wall — R-30 nominal, U ≈ 0.189 W/m²·K, graphite-grey EPS core · Expand for full spec
  • Panel assembly: 11 mm OSB skin / 143 mm graphite-grey EPS core / 11 mm OSB skin; nominal 165 mm (6.5") total thickness.
  • Core thermal conductivity λ ≈ 0.031 W/m·K (graphite EPS); nominal R-30 imperial.
  • Thermal: R_SI = R-30 × 0.1761 ≈ 5.28 m²·K/W → U ≈ 0.189 W/m²·K. Note: U ≠ λ. Meets OBC 9.36.2.6 above-grade wall, Climate Zone 6.
  • Panel joints: surface spline (2× OSB strips let into each skin) + continuous EPS fill; sealant bead on both sides of each joint.
  • Plates: double 38 mm SPF bottom plate seated on gasket over OSB subfloor; single 38 mm top plate; fasten panel to plates with 8 Ø × 200 mm SIP screws @ 150 mm o.c. per face.
  • Air barrier: exterior-side self-adhered WRB; interior continuity at plate-to-panel seam (OBC 9.25.3).
  • Vapour control: warm-side-ready per OBC 9.25.4; smart-retarder or poly VB depending on wall stackup.
  • Panel height 2438 mm (8'-0"); standard panel module width 1220 mm; wall runs split on stud-module centers.

Technical Note — Code & Reference

  • OBC 9.25.3 — Air barrier systems; continuity at plate/panel interfaces.
  • OBC 9.25.4 — Vapour barriers on warm-in-winter side.
  • OBC 9.25.5 — Thermal insulation continuity.
  • OBC 9.36.2.6 — Effective thermal resistance, Climate Zone 6 (above-grade wall).
  • CSA O437 — OSB facing grade.
  • SIP assembly requires CCMC evaluation for the panel series; verify active listing before specifying.
  • U-value derivation: U [W/m²·K] = 1 / (R_imp × 0.1761); do not confuse with core conductivity λ.

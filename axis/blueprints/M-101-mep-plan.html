Axis Modular SIP Series · M-101 — MEP Coordination Plan (Ground Floor)

Rev. 02 · 2026-04-19 · ISSUED FOR CONSTRUCTION
M-101: MEP coordination — 200 A service · ≈9000 BTU/h mini-split · HRV · PEX + ABS DWV · Expand for full spec
  • Electrical service: 200 A combination panel in bedroom closet (CSA C22.1); grid-connected single-phase 120/240 V.
  • Branch circuits: 2 × 20 A GFCI kitchen small-appliance; 1 × 20 A bath GFCI; 1 × 15 A lighting (entry + 3 zones, 3-way at entry); 4 × 15 A general receptacles per OBC 9.34 (≈1 per 3.6 m of usable wall); dedicated 15 A HRV; 15 A mini-split indoor; 20 A/240 V mini-split condenser.
  • Life safety: interconnected hardwired smoke alarms in bedroom, hall, and kitchen-adjacent living zone; CO alarm in hall within 5 m of bedroom door; battery backup per OBC 9.10.19.
  • HVAC — heating/cooling: wall-mounted ductless mini-split head ≈ 9 000 BTU/h (2.6 kW) nominal cooling / 12 000 BTU/h heating @ -15 °C; HSPF ≥ 10, SEER ≥ 20; insulated Cu refrigerant line set in interior ceiling chase to NE-corner exterior condenser.
  • HVAC — ventilation: HRV unit ≈ 60 L/s (≈ 125 cfm) continuous, balanced; 150 mm insulated flex/rigid supply ducts to LR + BR; 150 mm returns from kitchen (grease-screened) + bath; intake/exhaust through parapet with 1 m minimum separation per OBC 9.32.3.
  • Plumbing — water: 20 mm PEX cold/hot main from south-end service entry to bath manifold; 13 mm PEX home-runs to each fixture with ¼-turn shut-offs; water-heater type: electric storage tank (40 gal) or heat-pump water-heater — see P-501.
  • Plumbing — DWV: 75 mm ABS stack from bath group (WC, lav, shower) + kitchen sink; 50 mm trap arms; vent-through-roof at bath stack (see A-102 roof plan).
  • Chases: all horizontal refrigerant, duct, and branch circuits routed above ceiling in service cavity; vertical risers in interior partitions only (no SIP penetrations except intake/exhaust + VTR).

S-201: I-joist floor — 302 mm I-joists @ 406 o.c., clear span 3657 mm · Expand for full spec
  • Joists: 302 mm (11-7/8") engineered wood I-joists with sawn-lumber or LVL flanges and OSB web; generic product, CCMC-listed series.
  • Spacing: 406 mm (16") o.c. typical; confirmed against live-load 1.9 kPa + dead-load 0.6 kPa for L/480 deflection.
  • Rim board: 38 mm LVL-grade continuous at perimeter; receives joist-end bearing and closes the rim cavity.
  • Blocking: solid mid-span blocking row for lateral stability; additional blocking over bearing lines per manufacturer charts.
  • Subfloor: 18 mm OSB T&G, glued & screwed to joist top flange; fastener spacing 150 mm at edges / 300 mm in field.
  • Joist hangers: HDG galvanized top-flange or face-mount hangers at rim-to-joist connections; nails per hanger schedule.
  • Cavity insulation: mineral-fibre batt R-24 SI ≈ 4.23 m²·K/W, U ≈ 0.237 W/m²·K (see A-201 for full stackup).

Technical Note — Code & Reference

  • OBC 9.23 — Wood-frame construction: floor-joist spans and bearing.
  • CSA O437 — OSB (subfloor sheathing).
  • Manufacturer-specific span tables and hanger schedules govern; use listed CCMC evaluation for the engineered I-joist series selected.
  • Deflection limit L/480 under live load; vibration check per APA / CCMC design guide for long spans.

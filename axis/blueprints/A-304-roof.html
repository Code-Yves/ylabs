  • Perimeter HDG drip edge with hemmed outer lip; continuous cleat; mechanically fastened termination bar with sealant.
  • Air/vapour barrier on interior face of roof SIP continuous to wall SIP air barrier; sealed taped lap.

  • Fastener schedule per panel manufacturer; lifting and handling limits to be confirmed by Site Engineer.

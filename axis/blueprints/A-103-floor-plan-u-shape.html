A-103: U-Shape floor plan — 45'-0" × 57'-0" · 2 BDRM + Office · 2 BATH · Master Suite + Guest wings around a 945 SF south-opening courtyard · Expand for full spec
  • Overall exterior: 13 720 × 17 370 mm (45'-0" × 57'-0") — three Drop & Go modules (N connector + 2 wings) assembled on-site.
  • Enclosed area: ≈1 620 SF gross (45' × 12' north connector + 2 × 12' × 45' south wings). Courtyard: ≈945 SF open-air (21' × 45'), enclosed on three sides (N / E / W), open to south.
  • North connector (open-plan): Living Room · Kitchen (w/ island) · Dining Room; courtyard-facing south elevation glazed floor-to-ceiling in 3 bays separated by mullions.
  • West wing (master suite, N→S): Office/Bedroom · Bath 1 (SH+WC+Dbl Lav) · Walk-in Closet · Master Bedroom. Courtyard-facing FTC glazing at Office and Master BR; south wall FTC at master BR.
  • East wing (guest wing, N→S): Laundry/Mud Room · Bath 2 (SH+WC+Dbl Lav) · Walk-in Closet · Bedroom 2. Courtyard-facing FTC glazing at Laundry/Mud and BR 2; south wall FTC at BR 2. MAIN ENTRANCE on east wall @ Laundry/Mud (NE corner).
  • Envelope: 191 mm graphite-EPS SIP exterior walls, R-30 effective (U ≈ 0.189 W/m²·K); see A-301.
  • Glazing: FTC bays on courtyard + S bedroom walls = triple-pane IGU, argon fill, low-e; conventional punched windows on N/W/E outer walls. Per user spec, blue lines on plan indicate FTC glazing extent.
  • Partitions: 2×4 studs @ 406 o.c., gyp both sides, STC 35 min.; see A-401.
  • Plumbing: two wet rooms stacked centrally on each wing for efficient back-to-back riser; 1.28 gpf WCs, 1.5 gpm lav aerators, low-flow showers; see P-501.
  • Floor finishes: vinyl plank (Living/Kitchen/Dining/Office/Laundry/BRs), tile (baths), carpet (bedrooms optional); courtyard pavers on pedestals over drainage mat.
  • Courtyard landscape (L-101 toggleable layer): 12'-0" outdoor kitchen counter on west courtyard wall (grill + utility sink) + specimen tree centered near south end.
  • Ceiling height: 2438 mm (8'-0") clear; see sections A-201, A-301, A-304.

Technical Note — Code & Reference

  • OBC 9.5 — Design for habitation: minimum areas, natural-light & ventilation ratios per room; all habitable rooms meet glazing minima via FTC courtyard elevations + outer wall windows.
  • OBC 9.9 — Means of egress: primary egress via NE main entry door (east wing, through Laundry/Mud); secondary egress via courtyard FTC openings where operable (min. 1 per wing per 9.9.10.1).
  • OBC 9.10.9.14 / 9.10.9.16 — Fire separations not required within single-dwelling U-shape; verify if dual-occupancy (secondary suite) variant is selected.
  • OBC 9.10.19 — Smoke + CO alarms: one per bedroom + hallway + each wing; interconnected hard-wired with battery backup.
  • OBC 9.31 — Plumbing services: two-stack layout per P-501 — west wing bath stack + east wing bath stack.
  • OBC 9.32.3 — Mechanical ventilation (HRV): one HRV for main dwelling, supply to all habitable rooms, exhaust from wet rooms + kitchen per M-602.
  • OBC 9.36.2.6 / 9.36.2.8 — Effective thermal resistance, Climate Zone 6; continuous SIP envelope meets prescriptive path; FTC glazing U-value must meet trade-off per 9.36.5.
  • OBC 9.7.6 — Skylights / large glazing: FTC courtyard walls comply with 9.7 structural glazing tables; tempered safety glass at all FTC assemblies within 900 mm of floor.
  • Barrier-free: both baths 915 mm clear for aging-in-place option; 1525 mm turning radius provided in each bath, laundry, and master BR.

A-303: 914 × 2032 insulated steel door, U ≈ 1.1 W/m²·K · Expand for full spec
  • Door slab: 36″ (914) × 80″ (2032) × 1-3/4″ (44 mm); 0.8 mm steel skins over PU-foam core.
  • Frame: 16 ga HDG steel knock-down jamb set in wood buck; anchors at 3 hinges + 2 strike, 400 mm o.c. max.
  • Weather strip: kerf-in EPDM compression bulb on head and both jambs; magnetic sweep at bottom with ferrous threshold strip.
  • Threshold: aluminum, thermally broken, set on continuous sill pan; interior upturned dam.
  • Sill pan: self-adhered membrane, sloped outward ≥ 6°, back-dam 10 mm, end-dams at jambs.
  • Head flashing: Z-flashing hemmed drip; lapped behind WRB, sealant at joints.
  • Interior strike reinforcement: 3 mm steel backer plate full jamb height, 75 mm screws to buck.
  • Hardware: lever handle set + deadbolt; 3 hinges; all hardware finish schedule per architect.
  • Threshold height ≤ 13 mm above finish; barrier-free clear width ≥ 860 mm.

A-402: Entry stair · R = 197 · T = 255 · handrail 865–965 · headroom ≥ 1950 · Expand for full spec
  • Exterior entry stair, typ. 6–8 risers; closed stringers, closed risers.
  • Rise (riser) 197 mm max (7¾″); all risers equal within ±6 mm per OBC 9.8.3.
  • Run (tread) 255 mm min (10″); nosing projection ≤ 25 mm.
  • Handrail 865–965 mm above nosing line; graspable profile Ø 32–50 mm per OBC 9.8.10.
  • Guard at landing: top ≥ 1070 mm, picket clear < 100 mm.
  • Open risers prohibited where children may use the stair.
  • Headroom above nosing line ≥ 1950 mm (6′-5″).
  • Stringer: PT SPF 50×235, bearing on concrete pad or helical footing.
  • Landing: ≥ 900 mm deep in direction of travel, level ±2% for drainage.

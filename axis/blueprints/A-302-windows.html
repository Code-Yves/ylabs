Axis Modular SIP Series · A-302 — Triple-Pane Window Detail

Rev. 02 · 2026-04-19 · ISSUED FOR CONSTRUCTION
A-302: Triple-pane argon IGU, U = 0.90 W/m²·K · Expand for full spec
  • IGU: 4-16-4-16-4 mm triple pane; argon fill both cavities; low-e coatings on surfaces #2 and #5.
  • Warm-edge non-metallic composite spacer, desiccant-filled, continuous bead top & bottom.
  • Frame: multi-chamber extrusion with polyamide / glass-fibre thermal-break strut.
  • PVC nailing flange lapped over WRB; membrane tape sequenced sill → jambs → head.
  • Sill pan: self-adhered membrane, sloped outward ≥ 6°, with back-dam and end-dams.
  • Head flashing: Z-flashing with min. 6° slope, hemmed drip edge projecting beyond cladding.
  • Interior air seal: low-mod silicone bead over closed-cell backer rod; continuous at all 4 sides.
  • Shim space: window-rated low-expansion PU foam; no solid shims in load path of frame.
  • Window U-factor per CSA A440.2; Energy Rating target ≥ 34 for Climate Zone 6.

Technical Note — Code & Reference

  • OBC 9.7 — Windows, doors and skylights.
  • OBC 9.27.3 — Flashing installation at openings.
  • OBC 9.25.3 — Air barrier systems; continuity to WRB required.
  • OBC 9.25.4 — Vapour barrier on warm-in-winter side.
  • OBC 9.36.2.6 / 9.36.2.8 — Effective thermal resistance, Climate Zone 6.
  • CSA A440.2 — Fenestration energy performance.
  • U-factor formula: U [W/m²·K] = 1 / (R_imp × 0.1761).

S-202: Floor framing plan — 32 I-joists @ 406 o.c., clear span 3658 mm, 3 rows mid-span blocking · Expand for full spec
  • Joist layout: 32 engineered wood I-joists, 241 mm (9½") series, spanning the short (12'-0") module axis at 406 mm (16") o.c. typical.
  • Clear span: 3658 mm (12'-0") rim-to-rim; end bearing 38 mm minimum on engineered rim; verify per CCMC-listed manufacturer span chart.
  • Deflection: L/480 under live load (1.9 kPa residential), L/360 total load; vibration/bounce check per APA / CCMC design guide.
  • Rim board: 38 × 241 engineered rim (LVL-grade or equivalent) continuous at all perimeter edges; closes rim cavity and transfers shear.
  • Mid-span blocking: solid 38 × 241 blocks between joists at 3 lines across the 12.8 m length — bay lengths 3200 / 2440 / 2440 / 2720 mm, all ≤ 2440 mm (8') rule per OBC 9.23.6 intent.
  • Bearing: two long-axis HSS chassis beams (shown heavy dashed) provide interior bearing; joists are continuous over bearings, no splices within span.
  • Subfloor above: 19 mm (¾") OSB T&G, glued with elastomeric adhesive + screwed 150 edge / 300 field; diaphragm action per A-201.
  • Openings: none — single-level module, no stair opening or chase; all plumbing/mechanical penetrations as round web holes per note 6.
